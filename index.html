About Me & My Journey

Rooted in Sylhet’s Serenity, Thriving in Central London

My story began amidst the lush greenery and tranquil freshwater swamp forests of Ratargul, Gowainghat, Sylhet, Bangladesh. Growing up surrounded by natural beauty—from Ratargul to Jaflong and Bichnakandi—instilled in me a lifelong appreciation for nature, community, and resilience.

In January 2019, I relocated to the United Kingdom, embarking on a transformative chapter that has now spanned over 7 years and 10 months. Having earned British Citizenship, the UK is no longer merely where I reside—it is home, and the foundation where I am building my long-term future.

After pursuing my university education in Manchester starting in 2020, I moved to Central London in May 2023 to immerse myself in real estate development, strategic investments, and London’s dynamic international community.
